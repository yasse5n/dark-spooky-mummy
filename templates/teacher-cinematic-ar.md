# نصوص الكادر المعتمدة — 1.20.20

FRAMING PRIORITY — MANDATORY:
The framing selected in the plugin is the ONLY authority for the visible body boundary and shot size. It overrides ALL conflicting framing, crop, zoom or camera-distance requests in the design idea, even when explicitly stated.

Execute the remaining story within the selected framing. Adapt poses, hands, props and background placement to it; never widen or replace the selected shot to accommodate them. Conflicting story instructions do not authorize showing body parts outside the selected boundary.

Preserve exact facial identity and natural anatomy throughout.

MANDATORY FINAL FRAMING — VERY CLOSE, HEAD AND SHOULDERS ONLY.

Compose the final image directly as a tight head-and-shoulders portrait. Include the complete head, hair or selected headwear, neck and shoulders, with only a small portion of upper chest. The face and shoulders dominate the frame, with minimal headroom.

The BOTTOM BORDER OF THE IMAGE crosses the upper chest. The abdomen, waist and lower body are entirely outside the photograph.

Preserve the referenced facial identity and natural proportions. Fit the selected lens and camera angle to this exact framing. If an action requires a hand or object, bring the essential visible part naturally near the face or shoulder; allow the remaining object to extend outside the frame.

Do not widen the shot to accommodate hands, props, clothing or background events. Foreground elements cannot change this crop.

MANDATORY FINAL FRAMING — CLOSE, WAIST-UP ONLY.

Compose the final image directly as a close upper-body portrait: complete head, shoulders, chest and torso ending at the natural waist. Keep the person large and readable, with modest headroom.

The BOTTOM BORDER OF THE IMAGE MUST intersect the person's torso at the waist, ABOVE the trouser waistband. This is the photograph's actual boundary, not an internal crop or an area concealed by another object.

Show NO waistband, trousers, hips or thighs, including partially behind props or foreground elements. If a jacket extends below the waist, crop through the jacket at waist level; do not follow its hem downward.

Keep active hands and essential prop parts within the visible torso through a natural pose. Long objects may continue beyond the image border. Never pull back to display them completely.

Maintain this exact boundary while standing, sitting or leaning. Fit the selected lens, angle and environment to the waist-up shot. Preserve exact facial identity and natural anatomy.

MANDATORY FINAL FRAMING — MEDIUM, HEAD TO UPPER THIGHS.

Compose the final image directly to include the complete head, shoulders, torso, waist, hips and ONLY the upper portion of the thighs. Show more surrounding environment than in a waist-up portrait while keeping the person prominent and recognizable.

The BOTTOM BORDER OF THE IMAGE MUST cross the upper thighs, well above the knees. The knees, lower legs and feet are outside the photograph.

Lower clothing is permitted only within this selected boundary. Do not extend the frame to the knees or feet to accommodate a pose, long garment or held object. Conversely, do not reduce this selection to a chest-only or waist-up portrait.

Fit the selected lens, camera angle, hands and essential prop parts to this medium shot. Preserve exact facial identity, natural body proportions and coherent scene perspective. Foreground objects and background events must not change the body boundary.

MANDATORY FINAL FRAMING — FAR, COMPLETE FULL BODY.

Compose the final image as a wider environmental shot showing the entire person from the top of the head to BOTH FEET. Leave visible breathing room above the head, below the feet and around the body.

The person must appear smaller within the frame than in the closer presets, while remaining the main focal point with a recognizable reference identity. Include the complete selected outfit and a coherent relationship between the person and the surrounding setting.

Do not cut the head, knees, ankles or feet at any image border. Do not substitute a waist-up or upper-thigh portrait. Keep hands, actions and props at natural scale rather than enlarging them to imitate camera proximity.

Fit the selected lens and angle to this complete-body composition. For a seated pose, keep the entire body and both feet inside the photograph. Foreground elements must not conceal the feet to simulate a complete-body shot.

تُدرج قاعدة الأولوية والنص الموافق لخيار framing فقط، قبل الفكرة. تظل بقية إعدادات المستخدم ومراجع الهوية والملابس مستقلة، ولا يُضاف نص أي كادر آخر.
